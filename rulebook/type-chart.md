# Type Effectiveness Chart

Type matchups determine the effectiveness multiplier in damage calculation. The chart below shows effectiveness of the **attacking type** (rows) against the **defending type** (columns).

- **2.0**: Super-effective (twice damage).
- **1.0**: Neutral (normal damage).
- **0.5**: Not very effective (half damage).
- **0.0**: No effect (zero damage).

## Key Notes

- **Ghost vs. Normal**: 0.0. Ghost moves do not affect Normal types.
- **Poison vs. Steel**: 0.0. Poison moves do not affect Steel types.
- **Electric vs. Ground**: 0.0. Electric moves do not affect Ground types.
- **Dragon vs. Fairy**: 0.0. Dragon moves do not affect Fairy types.
- **Dual-type defenders**: Type effectiveness is multiplied for each type. Example: Fire move vs. Water/Rock = 0.5 × 2.0 = 1.0.

## Canonical Reference

This chart follows Generation 6+ (X/Y onward) type effectiveness, which has remained unchanged through Sword/Shield and Scarlet/Violet. This is the most recent and stable version of the type chart.
